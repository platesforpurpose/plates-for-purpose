( About )

Plates for Purpose is a McGill student initiative. We cook meals and deliver them to local shelters supporting people experiencing homelessness.

( How it works )
  1. 1FundEvents and donations pay for groceries and cooking supplies.
  2. 2CookStudent volunteers prepare meals off campus in shared kitchen sessions.
  3. 3DeliverVolunteers bring the finished meals to our partner shelters in Montreal.
( Partners )
  • MTLThe Open DoorA Montreal shelter supporting people experiencing homelessness.
